The question this answers that pLDDT cannot: is the peptide placed confidently relative to the groove — measured mean 2.2 Å peptide↔HLA, against 1.7 Å within the HLA itself.
Predicted structure, not an experimental observation. Confidence scores describe the model's self-assessment, not biological validity — we measured that a deliberately mismatched peptide/allele pair still scores ipTM 0.988, and structure predictors are known to dock non-binding peptides into the groove (Motmaen et al., PNAS 2023). Ranking comes from the binding screen.

Do not read binding strength off the picture. For a published wild-type/mutant pair (PDB 7L1B / 7L1C), a 70× difference in complex half-life corresponds to just 0.73 Å of peptide backbone RMSD. Binding lives in anchor chemistry and kinetics, which a static image cannot show — which is why this panel measures one named, pre-specified contact instead.
